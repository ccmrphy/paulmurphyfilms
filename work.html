Camera & Grip

Over more than four decades, Paul has worked as key grip and dolly grip across some of the world's most ambitious productions. Beginning on films like Goodbye Pork Pie and UTU in the early 1980s, he became one of New Zealand's most trusted grips — working on Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings trilogy and King Kong — and has continued to take on major international productions including The White Lotus, Our Flag Means Death, and The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power.
